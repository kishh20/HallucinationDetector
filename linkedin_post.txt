🚨 Generative AI models are brilliant, but they hallucinate with 100% confidence.

Whether it is fabricating academic citations, conflating historical events, or asserting faulty scientific proofs, hallucination remains the #1 bottleneck preventing enterprise and research adoption of LLMs.

To tackle this, I engineered Hallucination Detector — a real-time, web-grounded AI fact-checking engine that autonomously deconstructs and verifies claims before presenting them as truth.

Recently, I completely overhauled the entire architecture and frontend to production-grade engineering standards:

🧠 Autonomous Verification Architecture:
1️⃣ Query Distillation: Deconstructs complex, multi-clause prompts into semantic entity queries.
2️⃣ Parallel Web Grounding: Multi-threaded scraping and concurrent indexing from Wikipedia and live authoritative web sources.
3️⃣ Grounded Synthesis: Powered by Google Gemini (gemini-3.8-flash), generating structured responses strictly bound to retrieved empirical evidence.
4️⃣ Claim-Level NLI Verification: An autonomous verifier model (gemini-3.5-flash-lite) breaks down responses into atomic propositions, runs independent Natural Language Inference (NLI) entailment tests, and outputs an explainable Truth Score.
5️⃣ Complete Transparency: Expandable evidence citations, claim-by-claim breakdown, and real-time verification status.

⚡ What's New in Version 3.0:
• Ultra-fast FastAPI backend replacing legacy Streamlit for sub-second latency.
• Real-time SSE (Server-Sent Events) token streaming with live step-by-step progress indication.
• KaTeX LaTeX math rendering for flawless mathematical and scientific formula display.
• Voice Dictation with integrated Speech-to-Text for seamless conversational querying.
• Progressive Web App (PWA) support — fully installable on iOS, Android, and Desktop.
• Session persistence backed by SQLite with one-click Markdown & JSON report export.
• Sleek ChatGPT-grade dark UI (#212121) with responsive typography and interactive profile settings.
